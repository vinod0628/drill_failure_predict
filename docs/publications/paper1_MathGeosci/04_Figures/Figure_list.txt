    Figure 1 Structure of the prediction system. The geological model, the records of the current hole and the multi-area history feed a common feature engine (one row per meter). The trajectory and geological-model ensemble feeds Models H, A and B and the drilling-response rules R1–R8, which produce levels, warning zones with a ±u depth band, SHAP-based reasons and the probability of reaching the target. Finished holes return to the history and the models are retrained.
