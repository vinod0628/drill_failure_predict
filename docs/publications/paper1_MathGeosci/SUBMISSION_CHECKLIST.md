## Notes for the authors
- On this benchmark, the rock-mechanical descriptors had no measurable effect beyond the run-to-run spread (three
  seeds). The paper reports this openly. Consider whether the title should still name them. Alternative title:
  *"Transferable ahead-of-the-bit prediction of core-drilling failures with a discrete-time hazard model,
  geological-model uncertainty and explainable gradient boosting"*.
