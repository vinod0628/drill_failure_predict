## Checklist
- [x] Abstract as one paragraph of at most 200 words (200), structured as background, methods, results and
  conclusions; 8 keywords.
- [x] Structure: Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusions.
- [x] MDPI back matter: Author Contributions (CRediT), Funding, Institutional Review Board Statement, Informed
  Consent Statement, Data Availability Statement, Acknowledgments (with the generative-AI statement, tool named),
  Conflicts of Interest, Abbreviations.
- [x] Numbered references in order of citation, MDPI style; DOIs checked against Crossref.
- [x] Synthetic-benchmark status stated in the abstract, the methods, the captions, the limitations and the cover letter.
- [x] Companion manuscript (*Mathematical Geosciences*) disclosed in the cover letter and cited as "in preparation".
- [ ] Author names, affiliations, ORCID, corresponding e-mail; CRediT roles; funding.
- [ ] Select the journal section in SuSy (the cover letter suggests the section on exploration methods and applications).
- [ ] Suggested reviewers: confirm current affiliations and e-mails, and that there is no conflict of interest
  (MDPI asks for reviewers from different institutions and countries than the authors).
- [ ] Move the text into the MDPI *Minerals* Word template, downloaded from the journal page. MDPI can do this at
  production, but a template-formatted file speeds up the editorial check.
- [ ] When the companion paper gets a DOI, replace "companion paper (in preparation)" with the citation.
